Nandos Spicy Rice Recipe

Home

Description

Craving Nandos rice and want to replicate it at home for your next fakeaway? Look no further.

Ingredients

  • long grain rice
  • chicken - thighs preferable
  • red pepper
  • red onion
  • garlic
  • tomato pasatta
  • turmeric
  • cayenne pepper
  • paprika
  • vegetable stock
  • dressing of choice

Steps

  1. Season chicken thighs with a seasoning of choice and then pan fry until golden and cooked through. Once cooked, remove chicken thighs from the pan.
  2. Dice the onion, red pepper and garlic and add to the pan. Cook until softened.
  3. Add spices - turmeric, cayenne pepper, and paprika.
  4. Add vegetable stock and tomato passata.
  5. Add long grain rice - uncooked.
  6. Cover pan and leave for 20 minutes. After 20 minutes, check to ensure the rice is cooked.
  7. Once the rice is cooked, add the chicken thighs back to the pan and cover again with the lid - allowing the chicken thighs to warm through.
  8. Serve with a dressing of choice.

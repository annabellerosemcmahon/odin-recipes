Creamy Bacon Pasta Recipe

Home

Description

Enjoy this creamy and rich bacon pasta dish. Perfect for when you're feeling lazy and want to have a low maintenance, delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • smoked bacon
  • red onion
  • double cream
  • sun-dried tomatoes
  • white wine
  • pasta of choice
  • chicken stock
  • paramesan
  • garlic bread [optional]

Steps

  1. Prepare the ingredients by dicing the bacon, sun-dried tomatoes and red onion. Place pasta in boiling water and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Pan fry bacon until golden and red onion until soft
  3. Add sun-dried tomatoes.
  4. Add double cream and stock to the pan. Allow to simmer and thicken.
  5. Once the sauce is to your likening, add the cooked pasta.
  6. Plate and garnish with grated parmesan. Serve with garlic bread [if you decided to include it].

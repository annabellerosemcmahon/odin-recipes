Chicken Chorizo Gnocchi Recipe

Home

Description

This chicken and chorizo gnocchi recipe is warming, filling, and beaming with flavour thanks to the spicy chorizo. Perfect for an autumnal evening.

Ingredients

  • gnocchi
  • chicken
  • spicy chorizo
  • red onion
  • sun dried tomatoes
  • paprika
  • garlic
  • cayenne pepper
  • double cream
  • chicken stock
  • tomato puree
  • parmesan
  • garlic bread [optional]

Steps

  1. Dice and season chicken with seasoning of choice and add to a warm pan. Pan fry until brown and then remove and put to the side.
  2. Place pasta in salted boiling water and leave for 15 minutes.
  3. Dice red onion, sun-dried tomatoes and garlic and add to the pan - cook until softened.
  4. Add paprika, cayenne pepper and tomato puree.
  5. Add chicken stock and double cream, and allow to simmer.
  6. Grated parmesan into the sauce
  7. Add cooked pasta and stir to ensure all pasta is coated in the sauce.
  8. Plate and garnish with grated parmesan. Serve with garlic bread [if you decided to include it].
